Our story

One company for the whole to-do list

Most homes need more than one kind of work: a room to paint, a floor to replace, a driveway to pour, a railing to install, a wall that needs patching. Usually that means calling a different company for every job.

The idea behind Phoenix Delta is simple. It’s a new company that brings a wide range of installation and repair trades under one name and one phone number, so homeowners in the Jacksonville area can make one call and get many jobs handled.

Because the company is brand new, there’s no long list of past projects here yet. As the work gets done, real photos of it will fill this site.

The name

Why a phoenix?

The phoenix is the bird that rises renewed. That’s the idea behind the work: taking worn, tired or unfinished parts of a home and bringing them back better.

Rise

A brand-new company starting out in the Jacksonville area, ready to earn its name one job at a time.

Renew

Fresh paint, new floors, clean surfaces and solid finishes: the trades are all about making things new again.

Repair

Installation and repair. When something needs fixing, it’s one call to the same number.
