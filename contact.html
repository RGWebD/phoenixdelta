Questions

Before you call

What does Phoenix Delta do?

Home installation and repair across many trades: painting, plastering, caulking, interior remodeling (non-licensed work), cabinetry, handyman services, flooring, decorative stone, tile, marble, granite and terrazzo, stuccoing, pressure washing, driveway and tennis court installation, and canvas awning and ornamental iron installation.

Where do you work?

Jacksonville, Florida and the surrounding areas, including Duval, Clay, St. Johns and Nassau counties. Not sure if you’re in range? Call and ask.

How do I get an estimate?

Call 904-562-9356, or fill in the estimate form on the Contact page. It opens a pre-filled text message to 904-562-9356 on your phone, so nothing is sent until you press send.

Can you handle more than one job on the same visit?

That’s the idea behind Phoenix Delta: one call for many jobs. Tell us everything on your list when you call and we’ll talk it through.

Do you do electrical, plumbing or structural work?

Interior remodeling here covers non-licensed work only. Work that requires a state-licensed trade isn’t part of the service list.
